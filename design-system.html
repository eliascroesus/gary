Art direction

House rules

He is a warm, funny cartoon bird. Not a national symbol, not an alpha crest. When in doubt, make him scruffier.

Always

  • Thick solid ink outlines — 6px on the art, 5px on the UI.
  • Flat, saturated fills. The only gradients are skies.
  • Chunky, rounded, slightly wonky. Nothing ruler-straight.
  • Big expressive eyes with large black pupils.
  • Readable as a silhouette at 100px.
  • The single white feather, in every pose, every frame.
  • Cold to warm: storm, then river, then sunrise.

Never

  • Photorealism, heraldic crests, spread-wing flag poses.
  • Red-white-blue, stars and stripes, stone or metal textures.
  • Military or tactical styling. Grindset energy.
  • Majestic or intimidating. He’s scruffy and lovable.
  • Real photos, real people, brands, logos, film references.
  • Any hint that buying makes money. The eagle eats. The visitor is promised nothing.

Character

The young eagle

A juvenile bald eagle: mottled brown all over, feathers sticking out at wrong angles, talons clearly too big for him, a permanently hungry stare. One white feather from the very first frame.

Live rig

The real thing: one inline SVG with the canonical #eagle-* ids. Move your cursor — he watches it. Switch poses, turn his head white, make him hop.

Pose
0 / 6

The adult pose is always fully white.

  • Blinks at random every 3–5s, sometimes twice.
  • 2s breathing loop; wings sway, or flap when airborne.
  • Head tilts and pupils track the cursor; idle wander on touch.
  • Loose feathers lag and overshoot on every move.
  • Only rigs on screen animate. Reduced motion: he holds still.

Model sheet

Nine poses in story order, all at one world scale — the runt really is that small. Each sits on the sky of the scene it lives in, and every one of them is alive.

Plumage stages

Scene 6 and the milestone timeline. Each unlocked milestone turns one more patch white — crown, brow, back, eyes, nape, throat — and the beak ripens to yellow.

Silhouette test at 100px: every pose reads as a bird, and the signature feather survives in all nine.

Milestone timeline

A reusable component, fed by js/milestones.js. Horizontal on desktop, vertical on phones. Scroll through it: the path fills in, the rider flies to the last unlocked milestone, and each one pops with a feather burst.

Rig anatomy

Every pose is built from the same named, layered SVG groups in js/eagle.js. Each group carries data-part and its hinge in data-pivot. The live rig above animates exactly these groups.

  • #eagle-headHinges at the neck. Tilts toward the cursor.
  • #eagle-signature-featherThe one white feather. Lags and overshoots on every move.
  • #eagle-headfeathersSix white patches, .hf-1 … .hf-6, one per milestone.
  • #eagle-eyesWhites, lids, brows. Blinks every 3–5s.
  • #eagle-pupilsClipped to the whites; track the cursor in every scene.
  • #eagle-beakUpper + hinged lower mandible. Ripens to yellow.
  • #eagle-wing-l#eagle-wing-rRotate from the shoulder pivot.
  • #eagle-bodyTorso, belly patch, neck ruff. 2s breathing loop.
  • #eagle-talonsLegs and feet. Too big, on purpose.
  • #eagle-tailBehind everything, wags on landings.
